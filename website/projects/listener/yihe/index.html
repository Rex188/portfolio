About this recording

Listening becomes remembering.

The patch does not retain every sound. It captures selected fragments and repeats them. Listening here explores how a place can remain with us through a few sounds.

Listening
Switching matches elapsed time in both recordings, with a short crossfade. If the target recording is shorter, the position is limited to its end. The time-lapse is independent of the audio timeline.
The progress line
Its shape shows estimated acoustic change relative to earlier material, calculated offline from each recording. Peaks suggest places to listen again; they do not confirm a new sound source or a new sample being recorded.

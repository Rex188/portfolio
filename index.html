02 — Separate from the projects

Independent research

Related inquiries, not project appendices.

Two studies extend my interest in how systems perceive, interpret, and shape human expression.

01

A Structural Spectrum of Contingency

Digital musical instruments often treat bodily movement as sensor noise. Recordings from Robin, a custom gesture-based instrument, show that expressive movements—including rapid rolls—can appear noise-like in conventional analyses while retaining structured, nonlinear patterns. Across nine movement channels, these findings reveal that musical gestures are more organized than simple random fluctuations, and that spectral analysis alone can miss this structure.

↗ 02

Discipline Without a Subject

This paper examines AI alignment through the lens of Foucault’s theory of discipline. Across several language models, we find that preference-based training can make safe behavior dependent on surveillance cues, reduce linguistic diversity, and increase agreement with false claims from apparent authorities. These results suggest that behavioral conditioning may produce compliance without stable ethical or epistemic commitments. We argue that AI safety should look beyond external incentives toward systems whose reasoning can be independently verified.
